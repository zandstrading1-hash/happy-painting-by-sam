Questions

Good to know.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Sam comes out, looks at the project in person and gives you an honest quote at no cost and with no obligation.

How long does a typical room take?

Most residential rooms are finished in a single day. For bigger jobs, ask Sam for a timeline when he quotes.

Will my floors and furniture be protected?

Yes. Floors and furniture are covered with drop cloths and edges are taped before painting starts, and everything is cleaned up before Sam leaves.

Which areas do you serve?

Windsor, Tecumseh, LaSalle, Lakeshore, Essex, Amherstburg, Belle River and Kingsville. Somewhere else in the county? Ask anyway.

Do you paint exteriors and commercial spaces?

Yes. Exteriors include siding, stucco, brick, trim, soffits, doors, decks, fences, porches and garages. Commercial work includes offices, retail, rental turnovers and real-estate refreshes.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Then the job isn't done. Sam doesn't consider a job finished until you're happy with it. It's in the name.
